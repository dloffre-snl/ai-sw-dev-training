**Software Development with AI: A VR/Software Understanding Focus**

AI coding tools have fundamentally changed how software gets built, and it happened fast. Our VR and Software Assessment workflows stand to benefit enormously, but many of us haven't had the time, access, or environment to seriously explore what's possible. This course aims to fix that.

**What is this?**

A hands-on, lab-heavy training course where you'll use frontier AI coding tools (Cursor, Open Code, Copilot, and more) to build real software. This is not a lecture series on how AI works — it's a week of actually using these tools under guidance, so you can integrate them into your daily work.

You'll learn to:
- Go from idea to working prototype using AI-assisted development
- Understand what's happening under the hood (tool calls, RAG, prompt engineering, chain of thought)
- Write code that lasts — not just vibe-coded demos
- Recognize and mitigate the security risks of AI-assisted development
- Choose the right tool and approach for the task at hand

The first week is structured instruction and lab time - lectures, exercises, and guided
exploration of AI coding tools. The optional second week is dedicated project time where you
apply what you learned to your own research.

**Who should apply?**

You write tools or scripts as part of your VR, software assessment, or R&D work, and you want to get significantly more productive. You're an early adopter willing to experiment. You don't need AI experience - you need coding experience and curiosity.

**You must come with a specific coding task or project idea** that you'll develop during the course. This is your sandbox - bring something real that matters to you.
