# Course Feedback Summary — SNL AI SW Dev Training

Source: `~/Downloads/SNL AI SW Dev Training Feedback (Responses) - Form Responses 1.tsv` (8 responses, 5/22/2026 – 6/1/2026)

## Overall ratings (1–5)

3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5, 5, 4 → **avg 4.4** (5 of 8 gave a 5)

## Most valuable

- **Hands-on time with frontier models** in a sequestered setting (~5 mentions)
- **Instructor fluency / lived experience** with the tools and methodology
- **Walkthroughs of process patterns** — brainstorming, planning, skills, multi-agent orchestration
- Working on **their own project** with instructor interaction
- Realization of the **capability leap** frontier models have made in the last 6 months

## What didn't land

- **Hitting session/context limits** late in the week (2 mentions) — suggestion: move to Pro/$100 tier next time
- **Jupyter notebook exercises felt like an afterthought** — wanted them emphasized more (work first, then discuss)
- **Not enough structured exercises**; strategies discussed were hard to put into action
- **LLM/agent background** felt thin for less-experienced participants
- **Local-model examples** got skipped because models weren't pre-downloaded
- Cursor; bright room lights

## Other notes

- Strong endorsement: "should be mandatory for every Sandian who touches software development… mission-threatening lag" between frontier capability and collective understanding
- Several want **more time** (another week to four)
- Suggestion: **record the course** with topic callouts/timestamps
- Behavior change reported: shifting from "wait until approved" to active use
- Meta: one respondent pushed back on the single-answer format — felt reductive

## Actionable takeaways for next offering

1. Upgrade plan tier to avoid context/session limits
2. Pre-stage local model downloads before class
3. Add more structured exercises; restructure Jupyter notebooks as do-then-discuss
4. Add a short LLM/agent primer for less-experienced attendees
5. Consider recording with chapter markers
